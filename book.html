Cryptid Hunt

The Clue Book

Every clue a hunter can be dealt, in all. Each player holds one, and together the clues in a game point to exactly one space.

Reading the clues

  • "Within" includes the space itself. A forest space is within 1 space of forest.
  • Any matching feature counts. One nearby standing stone is enough, even if others are far away.
  • Colour clues count both structure types. A violet standing stone and a violet shack are both violet structures.
  • Stone and shack clues ignore colour. A standing stone of any colour counts as a standing stone.
  • "Not" clues only appear in Advanced games, and each of those games has exactly one.
  • Not every clue appears on every map. Clues that would fit too few or too many spaces on that map are left out, and no two players in a game share a clue.
